About Abdullahi Hassan

Founder / CEO
“Do what you say. Every time.”

Abdullahi Hassan is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Danzulai Petroleum Ltd, an oil and gas company based in Kano, Nigeria. He leads the business with a simple standard: honest prices, safe operations and promises that are kept.

Under his leadership Danzulai supplies fuel, LPG, lubricants and petrochemicals, and offers aviation fueling, crude oil trading, vessel chartering, storage and haulage to customers across Nigeria.
