DANZULAI PETROLEUM LTD - WEBSITE
================================
Pages (open index.html to start):
  index.html    Home
  about.html    About us
  team.html     Our team (people behind the pumps)
  blog.html     Blog & news
  contact.html  Contact, quote form and map

All pages are plain HTML with no build step. Upload the whole folder to any web host
(cPanel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, etc.) and point your domain to it.

THINGS TO REPLACE
- Team photos: add images in images/team/ (see README inside that folder).
- Team names, bios, Facebook / LinkedIn links on team.html.
- Blog posts on blog.html are samples. Reactions and comments need a comments service
  (or a CMS such as WordPress) when hosted on your own domain.
- The quote form prepares a WhatsApp / email message (no server needed).
- The hero slideshow uses drawn illustrations. Real photos can replace them.

SEO & SECURITY (added)
- BEFORE GOING LIVE: replace https://www.danzulai.com with your real domain everywhere
  (every .html file, sitemap.xml and robots.txt). Use find-and-replace on the whole folder.
- New pages: services.html (all 10 services), abdullahi-hassan.html, muhammad-abubakar.html, 404.html.
- Every page has its own title, description, canonical link, Open Graph / Twitter card and JSON-LD
  structured data (Organization, Service, FAQ, Person, Breadcrumb).
- Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; claim your Google Business Profile.
- Security headers: .htaccess (Apache/cPanel) and _headers (Netlify / Cloudflare Pages). Use whichever matches your host.
  Every page also carries a Content-Security-Policy and referrer policy in its <head>.
- .well-known/security.txt tells researchers how to report a problem.
